ABOUT

One person, one journey at a time

I'm not a used-car lot or an import agency. I import vehicles one at a time, to a specific order, and I go through the whole process with you — from choosing the trim to the number plate.

The upside is that you always know who you're talking to. The downside is that I don't have ten cars in a hall you could drive away today.

HOW I WORK

AFTER HANDOVER

And then?

This is the question I get most often, and it deserves an honest answer.

Avatr does not yet have an official service network in Slovakia. That means service and spare parts are handled individually — and I will tell you exactly how before you sign the contract, not after.

If a service network near your home is a requirement for you, I'll tell you straight that this car isn't for you yet.
