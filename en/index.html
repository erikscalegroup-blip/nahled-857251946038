WHY AVATR

A brand built by three

Avatr is not a startup. It was founded as a joint venture of three companies, each contributing what it does best — Changan the manufacturing and chassis, Huawei the electronics and driver assistance, CATL the batteries.

In Europe the brand is only just starting. That is exactly the moment when you can own a car you won't meet on the road.

THE VEHICLE'S JOURNEY

The whole journey, including the awkward parts.

Import in detail

The whole process usually takes several months. The exact date depends on the model, availability and customs clearance — and I'll tell you before you sign anything.

  1. 1

    Choice and reservation

    We choose the model, trim and colour. Your reservation places you in the queue.

  2. 2

    Availability check

    I confirm with the supplier that the exact configuration is available and give you a binding price calculation.

  3. 3

    Contract and deposit

    We sign a contract with the price itemised — vehicle, shipping, duty, VAT, homologation.

  4. 4

    Order in China

    The vehicle is ordered from the supplier. From this moment production and dispatch time runs.

  5. 5

    Sea freight

    The longest part of the journey. The vehicle is insured throughout and you know where it is.

  6. 6

    EU customs

    Clearing import duty and VAT on entry into the European Union.

  7. 7

    Homologation

    Approval of the vehicle for use in the Slovak Republic and issuing of documents.

  8. 8

    Handover

    Registration, number plate and handover of the vehicle.

AFTER HANDOVER

And then?

This is the question I get most often, and it deserves an honest answer.

Avatr does not yet have an official service network in Slovakia. That means service and spare parts are handled individually — and I will tell you exactly how before you sign the contract, not after.

If a service network near your home is a requirement for you, I'll tell you straight that this car isn't for you yet.

FAQ

What people ask

Is the reservation binding?

No. The reservation places you in the queue and costs nothing. An obligation only arises when you sign the purchase contract, where the price is itemised.

Can the car be registered in Slovakia?

A vehicle from the Chinese market needs approval for use in the Slovak Republic. It is part of what I arrange, and it is a line in the calculation — not a surprise at the end.

Why are there no prices on the website?

Because they wouldn't be true. The price depends on the exchange rate, duty, VAT and the specific trim. You get a calculation for your model free and without obligation.

Where will I service the car?

Avatr does not yet have an official service network in Slovakia. Service and parts are handled individually and I'll tell you exactly how — before signing, not after.

How long does it take?

Usually several months. I can give a more precise date only for a specific model and a specific shipping date.

Can I see the car before buying?

Not yet — I import cars to order and have no stock vehicles. That's why everything on this site is built so you know exactly what you're getting into.
